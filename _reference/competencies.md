# Competency framework

Replace this with your organization's PM framework. Each `## ` heading below is a
competency label: use the exact same text in a journal entry's `competency:` field, e.g.
`competency: ["Product sense", "Execution"]`. `_bin/vault-lint.py` reads these headings
to flag competencies with little evidence.

<!-- The labels below are a generic starting point, not any company's framework. -->

## Product sense
Understands customers and the market; picks the right problems; sound product judgment.

## Strategy
Sets direction, makes trade-offs explicit, connects work to business outcomes.

## Execution
Plans and ships reliably; manages scope, risk, and dependencies.

## Data and analytics
Defines success metrics, uses data to decide, and closes the loop after launch.

## Communication
Writes and presents clearly; tailors the message to the audience.

## Leadership and influence
Aligns people without authority; builds trust; develops others.
